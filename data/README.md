# Source data and provenance

The portfolio is based on publicly available **NERC BEDC monthly estimated-billing energy-cap publications for October and November 2026**.

No raw PDF files, complete extracted tables or utility internal records are redistributed here. The root [README](../README.md#source-publications) links to original NERC materials.

**Publication month is not necessarily the consumption reference month**: the November 2026 publication reports October 2026 as the reference period.

Published estimated-billing energy caps are **not** measured feeder energy consumption or delivered energy.
